## About evroc
evroc was founded to build Europe's first sustainable hyperscale cloud and AI infrastructure, and to enable a secure and sovereign digital future for Europe.

## The story behind evroc
Something fundamental is shifting in how the world competes. For decades, digital infrastructure was treated as a commodity, sourced wherever it was most convenient. European businesses, governments and institutions built their operations on platforms controlled by a handful of companies headquartered far beyond European borders. It worked well enough. Until it didn't.

Today, the cloud is not just infrastructure. It is the foundation on which AI is built, on which industries are transformed and on which strategic advantage is won or lost. And Europe, despite its deep reserves of engineering talent, industrial strength and scientific capability, finds itself dependent on non-European platforms to run its most sensitive workloads, train its frontier models and operate the services its societies depend on.

This is not a technical problem. It is a structural one, and it demands a structural answer.
evroc was founded to provide that.

## The Platform
Founded in 2022 and backed by European investors EQT Ventures, Norrsken VC, Giant Ventures and blisce, evroc is building what Europe has never had: a cloud and AI services platform at scale, designed, owned and operated under European jurisdiction, with no foreign control planes, no external software dependencies and no back doors that Europe didn't build itself.
When the legal or geopolitical climate shifts, control that sits outside Europe becomes a liability. evroc is built differently. Every layer of the stack, infrastructure, software and services, is under European ownership and control. That is what sovereignty actually means.
The platform launched in 2025 and is already serving clients in sensitive and highly regulated sectors, and is built for the environments where the stakes are highest: public institutions, defence organisations and operators of critical national infrastructure. On top of it sit evroc's own AI offerings, the enterprise AI agent roc and the sovereign AI platform evroc Think, which give organisations access to frontier-scale models without their data ever leaving European jurisdiction.

evroc operates active cloud regions from data centres in Stockholm, Paris and Frankfurt, with development teams across Sweden, France and the United Kingdom.

## Sustainability
Building the cloud of the future means rethinking how it is built. evroc's data centers are designed from the ground up for sustainability: powered by renewable energy, engineered for efficiency and built to minimize environmental impact at every stage. The goal is to scale at the pace the AI era demands, without compromising the planet.

## Ecosystem
evroc is building the first true European hyperscale cloud: technologically competitive with global leaders, but grounded in European values of sovereignty, security and sustainability. It is not building it alone. A sovereign digital future depends on an ecosystem, European investors, partners, developers, enterprises and public institutions, working towards the same goal.
By keeping critical infrastructure, innovation, and expertise within Europe, evroc strengthens the continent’s digital resilience and its capacity to compete. And by giving the next generation of European companies a sovereign foundation to build on, it helps them innovate faster, scale with confidence and unlock the full potential of AI, on infrastructure they can trust.
